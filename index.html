One More Room

A Halloween board game for two to four pieces — people, pairs of teammates, or bots. There is one life in the haunted mansion: one piece holds it, everyone else is a ghost trying to steal it. Hold it when the bell rings to score. Ten rounds.

Play on this screen Join on phone

This site plays on one screen with mouse, keyboard and bots. Phone controllers need the small room service running on a computer on your home Wi-Fi (see the README); phones then scan the QR code that computer shows.
